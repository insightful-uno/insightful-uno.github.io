    About

    Matthew Suggett is an illustrator and creative producer in Austin. He spent four years making co-branded campaign work as a Senior Production Artist at GoPro, ran the music licensing company Three's In Company, and led creator and brand partnerships at Epidemic Sound.

    He trained in visual effects in the Bay Area, releases music with the Soulection collective, and hosts Drawn Together, a live figure drawing series in Austin.
